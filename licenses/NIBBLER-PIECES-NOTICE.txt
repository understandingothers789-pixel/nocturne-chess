# Nibbler / shaded Merida pieces

The twelve PNGs in assets/pieces/nibbler are copied verbatim from:
https://github.com/rooklift/nibbler/tree/6eb0f066402c0cf53d08d682dbc394acd9cb5fb9/files/src/pieces
Nibbler repository license: GPL-3.0; see licenses/GPL-3.0.txt.
Nibbler's README credits Lichess for the piece artwork.

Merida: Armando Hernandez Marroquin; shaded artwork: Felix Kling.
Lichess lists Merida under GPL-2.0-or-later:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/cd2656d2787a766ba743da8da1dd3b24975e6329/COPYING.md
Editable upstream SVGs are included in assets/pieces/nibbler/source from:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/cd2656d2787a766ba743da8da1dd3b24975e6329/public/piece/merida
See licenses/MERIDA-GPL-2.0-or-later.txt.

The original PNGs retain their names and bytes: uppercase KQRBNP are White;
an underscore prefix denotes Black. No artwork edits. Since 0.5.7.18 the app
renders the matching shaded Merida SVGs directly at native device resolution,
including high-DPI displays, keeping the original Nibbler design and gradients.
The 240x240 PNGs remain included as archival references, not runtime textures.
No endorsement by Nibbler, Lichess or the artists is implied.
